## Scope
Landing page `public/index.html` (Persuade) and the studio `public/studio.html` (Operate) share one world. Visitor: founders, marketers, creators with a link, article or topic and no motion designer. Action: start a film from the hero composer. Proof: real films made by the studio, playing live, with their measured scores.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The studio is a film cutting bench flattened into a page. Every surface hangs off one perforated rail where scenes are frames on the beat; the category default it refuses is a dark SaaS page with a gradient hero, a demo video and a row of feature cards.
OWN-WORLD: True black ink (#0A0A0A) and charred black panels; grain-orange (#FF5A1F, coarse film grain) owns about a third of the landing; punched-white windows carry running copy and every film. One condensed grotesk in caps for labels, headlines and buttons (rank by cell count, not size); a readable semi-condensed grotesk for running text; mono only for timecode and measurements, in tabular columns. State is a mark, not a hue: grease cross = approved, tape flag = playhead, punched corner = unavailable, hung on a pin = earlier version. Buttons are perforated tabs.
STORY: Visitors see a real film cutting itself on the rail, understand that a crew of agents writes, designs, scores and cuts the film and measures it before anyone sees it, and start their own by pasting a link.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 40%: headline in condensed caps, "YOUR IDEA." / "CUT ON THE BEAT." (second line orange), two lines of running copy, then the working composer (link, article or topic) with the CUT MY FILM tab. Right 60%: the orange rail band with beat ruler and tape flag, a punched-white window playing a showcase film live from the engine, scene frames strung along the rail below it, earlier takes hung on pins.
FORM: Film cutting bench select rail (challenger "operate-a-cutting-bench-select-rail", won on audience and clarity over my seventh candidate, the cinema letter board); seed key 06e4be26. Raise from ikeda-datamatics: measurements set as dense tabular numerals. Raise from the letter board: one type size per rank across the page.
SIGNATURE INTERACTION: Scrubbing the rail with inertia that settles on beat pitch, never between beats; the tape flag follows the playhead; frames earn a grease cross when the critic approves them.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
